Why an ATS-friendly CV

A good-looking CV can still be rejected.

Fancy columns, graphics, text boxes and unusual headings can confuse screening software, so your experience never gets read. A clear structure fixes that.

  • Standard headings and a single-column layout that software can parse
  • Keywords matched to the role you are applying for
  • Achievement-focused bullet points that stand out to human readers
  • Gulf-specific details handled properly, such as visa status and nationality

FAQ

Common questions.

What does ATS-friendly mean?

ATS stands for applicant tracking system, the software many employers use to scan and rank CVs. An ATS-friendly CV uses a simple layout, standard headings and relevant keywords so the software can read it correctly.

How long does it take?

Your first draft is ready in 1 to 2 working days after you send your details.

Which file formats do I get?

You receive an editable Word file and a PDF, so you can update it yourself later.

Should I include a photo, nationality or visa status?

It depends on the employer and industry. We advise you on what is normal for your role and the Gulf market, then format it so it still works with screening software.

Can you guarantee I will get a job?

No. We cannot guarantee interviews or offers, but a clear, targeted CV gives you a much stronger start.

Do you keep my information private?

Yes. Your details are used only to prepare your documents and are not shared with anyone else.
